## 1 Summary

On 2024-09-02 an upper bench of the Tekri Quarry face (SL-TH-1, project PR-TQ) in Tekri Heights (Z-TH) slid after
heavy rain. About 900 m3 of debris crossed Tekri Quarry Road (RD-11) and washed into the D-7 head reach, which
stayed 15 % blocked for two weeks. RD-11 was blocked for 96 hours. Twelve houses in Tekri village (RA-22) were
evacuated, 60 people in total. Two quarry workers were injured and nobody died. About 300 people were affected.
Damage was estimated at 16 million.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

RG-01 on Tekri Hill recorded 260 mm over 72 hours and 140 mm in the 24 hours before the slide, with a peak
intensity of about 33 mm/h. SM-04, installed on the quarry crest earlier in 2024, read 0.80 saturation on the
morning of 2024-09-02 and had passed 0.70 the previous evening. The Kalinadi stood at 3.6 m on RV-01, below flood
stage. Runoff from the quarry floor carried the finer debris down into the D-7 head reach.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

The quarry face on SL-TH-1 had a mean angle of 55 degrees and a maximum of 70. It had been excavated to 35 m of a
planned 45 m. Since quarrying began in 2019, vegetation cover on the face had fallen from 65 % to 30 %. The
PR-TQ hazard review was still pending. RD-11 had not yet been regraded or given side ditches. There were no
debris traps between the quarry and D-7.

## 4 Impact

About 900 m3 of weathered quartzite and soil slid from the upper bench. Debris blocked RD-11 for 96 hours, and
runoff carried much of it into the D-7 head reach, where the channel stayed 15 % blocked for about 336 hours.
Two workers on the lower bench were injured. Four houses in RA-22 had minor damage from mud and stones, and 60
residents of the 12 nearest houses were evacuated. Quarry operations stopped.

## 5 Response

The quarry operator stopped work and called the control room. C-4 arrived within 30 minutes, rescued the two
injured workers and inspected the face for further movement. C-5 took the injured to H-1. The Incident
Commander ordered the 12 nearest houses in RA-22 evacuated, and about 45 people used S-6. C-3 cleared RD-11
over 96 hours and then removed debris from the D-7 head reach over two weeks.

## 6 Outcome

No one died. The two injured workers recovered. RD-11 reopened after 96 hours. The D-7 head reach was cleared
after about two weeks, and the channel's blocked fraction went back to its previous level. RA-22 residents
returned after 9 days, once C-4 found no further movement on the face. The Town Planning Department ordered a
hazard review for PR-TQ, and quarrying stayed suspended for about 30 days.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended completing the PR-TQ hazard review before any further deepening of the face. It
also recommended drainage for each quarry bench and catch fences above RD-11. RD-11 should be regraded with side
ditches. The Board asked for debris traps between the quarry floor and the D-7 head reach. SM-04 readings should
be linked to a rule for stopping quarry work during heavy rain.
