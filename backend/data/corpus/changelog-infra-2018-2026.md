This change log is maintained by the NMC Engineering Department and records every change to city
infrastructure, land use and slopes that alters drainage capacity, runoff, slope condition or emergency
capability. Each entry gives the change id, effective date, asset, the value before and after the change, and
the hazard review status. A hazard review is the hydraulic or slope review required before works are approved;
"not applicable" means the entry records a failure, survey finding or encroachment that was not an approved work.

## 2018 Changes in 2018
