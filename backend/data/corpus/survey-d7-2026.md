This report records the physical condition of the Kalinadi drain (D-7) before the 2026 monsoon. The NMC
Drainage Division walked the full 4.8 km channel between 18 and 22 May 2026, measured silt depth every 100 m,
photographed the lining and structures, and checked the channel level sensor CL-D7. The survey records
condition only. Flows and capacities are assessed in the hydraulic capacity study hydraulics-d7-2026.

## 1 Channel description

D-7 is a concrete-lined channel of trapezoidal section, built in 1994 and relined in 2016. It collects runoff
from Hillview (Z-HV) and from the western flank of Tekri Heights, runs south below the Hillview Terrace slopes,
passes under Hill Road (RD-01) through the BR-4 box culvert, crosses Riverside (Z-RS), receives the Riverside
outfall drain D-8 and discharges to the Kalinadi (R-1). For this survey it is divided into three reaches: the
head reach (about 1.4 km, from the head of the lining to the foot of SL-HV-1), the Hillview reach (about
1.0 km, to BR-4) and the Riverside reach (about 2.4 km, from BR-4 to the outfall). The asset register records a
design capacity of 42 m3/s and a current capacity of 27 m3/s, set by the BR-4 culvert since May 2025.

## 2 Head reach

The lining of the head reach is intact apart from hairline cracks in two wall panels. The invert carries a thin
layer of quartzite gravel and fines washed down from Tekri Heights, with a mean depth of 0.06 m. The debris that
entered this reach from the Tekri Quarry bench slide of September 2024 (HI-2024-LS-01) was removed within two
weeks of the event, and no residual obstruction was found. There are no debris traps between the Tekri Quarry
(PR-TQ) and the head of the channel. The trash rack at the head of the lining was clear, and its bars were
straight and firmly fixed.

## 3 Hillview reach

This reach runs below the three mapped Hillview slopes. The toe of the Hillview Terrace slope SL-HV-1, where
the Hillview Terrace Phase 2 excavation (PR-HT2) is in progress, lies about 60 m from the channel bank; the toe
of the Phase 1 cut SL-HV-2, behind its 2022 retaining wall, about 140 m; and the Hill Road cut SL-HV-3, under
rockfall mesh since 2020, about 20 m. Six surface drain outlets from the slopes enter the channel; two were
partly blocked with leaves and litter and were cleared during the survey. The Kalinadi Drain settlement (RA-02)
lines the banks close to BR-4, with houses about 5 m from the channel edge. Excavators can reach the channel
only from Hill Road at BR-4 and from a service ramp below SL-HV-2.

## 4 BR-4 culvert

The box culvert carrying Hill Road over D-7 was rebuilt in May 2025 during the Hill Road Widening (PR-HRW) as a
single cell 2.4 m wide and 1.8 m high, 14 m long, with a waterway area of 4.32 m2. It replaced the original
3.2 x 2.4 m opening of 7.68 m2. The new concrete is in good condition and the barrel was free of silt. A steel
trash rack stands about 8 m upstream of the inlet and was clear at the survey. Upstream of the inlet the lined
channel narrows from its full width to the culvert opening. The CL-D7 sensor is mounted on the upstream
headwall.

## 5 Riverside reach and outfall

Below BR-4 the channel crosses Riverside in a wider lined section. The lining is in fair condition, with minor
scour at the toe of the outer bank on two bends. The Riverside outfall drain D-8 joins about 0.5 km above the
outfall through a flap gate that closes when the Kalinadi reaches 4.0 m on RV-01. D-8 itself has been narrowed
by the D-8 settlement (RA-05, CH-2025-05), but no structures encroach on D-7 in this reach. The Riverside Old
Village (RA-03) lies about 200 m from the channel. The outfall apron on the Kalinadi bank is sound and was free
of sand at the survey.

## 6 Silt, debris and blockage

Mean silt depths were 0.06 m in the head reach, 0.04 m in the Hillview reach and 0.09 m in the Riverside
reach, and no measurement exceeded 0.20 m. The channel was last desilted in May 2024. The survey assessed the
blocked fraction of the channel as 0.00: no debris, fallen trees or dumped material obstructed the flow area at
any point. Household waste found along the RA-02 banks was removed during the survey. Both trash racks, at the
head of the lining and upstream of BR-4, were clear.

## 7 Monitoring equipment

The channel level sensor CL-D7 on the BR-4 headwall reports the water depth above the culvert invert, together
with the flow it indicates. During the survey it was checked against a temporary staff gauge and agreed within
0.02 m. CL-D7 is the only level sensor on D-7: there is none on the Riverside reach or at the outfall, where the
Kalinadi stage is read from RV-01 at the Kalinadi Bridge (BR-1). The rain gauge RG-02 and the soil moisture
probes SM-01 and SM-02 on SL-HV-1 are maintained by NMC-DMC and were not part of this survey.
