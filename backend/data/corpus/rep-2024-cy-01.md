## 1 Summary

Cyclone Meher crossed Nandipur from 2024-10-27 to 2024-10-29. It brought 190 mm of rain in 24 hours at RG-04 and
winds of 95 km/h gusting to 125 km/h at WS-01. Lakeside (Z-LK) was worst hit. Fixed pump PU-L2 failed on
2024-10-28, and D-11 capacity fell from 12 to 9.5 m3/s. PS-5, which feeds the D-11 pumps, stayed in service
throughout. About 28,000 people were affected, 6,000 were evacuated, 4 died and 48 were injured. Damage was
estimated at 210 million.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

The cyclone arrived on a wet post-monsoon catchment, with the Nandi Lake already high on LL-01. RG-04 recorded
310 mm over 72 hours, 190 mm of it in 24 hours, with a peak intensity of about 41 mm/h. WS-01 winds passed
89 km/h on 2024-10-27 and peaked at 95 km/h with gusts to 125 km/h. The Kalinadi rose to 4.9 m on RV-01. That
is above the 4.5 m level at which floodplain shelters are not activated, but below the 5.0 m BR-1 closure
stage.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

D-11 was at its 12 m3/s design capacity because PU-L3 had been replaced in 2023. With the river high, the DM-2
gates were closed and gravity outflow was unavailable, so D-11 relied on its three fixed pumps (PU-L1, PU-L2,
PU-L3). The pumps are fed from PS-5, which stands on a platform raised 1.2 m. H-4 is also supplied from PS-5.
S-7 lies on FP-4. BR-2 on RD-12 gave a second road crossing of the river.

## 4 Impact

PU-L2 failed on 2024-10-28, and D-11 surcharged for about 40 hours at 9.5 m3/s. RA-14 flooded to 0.6 m and RD-07
was closed for 48 hours. H-4's ground floor took 0.3 m of water. PS-5 held, but PS-2 lost power for 8 hours
because of feeder faults. BR-1 was closed for 20 hours. About 3,900 houses were damaged and 110 destroyed. Four
people died, three from falling trees and one in floodwater in Lakeside.

## 5 Response

Evacuation began 30 hours before landfall. Because RV-01 was forecast above 4.5 m, the Duty Officer did not open
S-7 and used S-1 (about 780 people) and S-3 (about 1,450) instead. C-7 moved Lakeside residents by boat and bus.
C-8 gave door-to-door warnings and laid sandbags in New Colony. After PU-L2 failed, C-2 placed mobile pumps
PU-M1 and PU-M2 at the D-11 sump. While BR-1 was closed, traffic used BR-2.

## 6 Outcome

The winds eased on 2024-10-29. The river fell below flood stage the following day and the DM-2 gates reopened,
and Lakeside drained over about three days. H-4 restored its ground-floor services after cleaning. PS-2 was
restored after 8 hours. PU-L2 was taken out of service and not repaired by the time of this review, so D-11
remained at 9.5 m3/s. All four deaths were recorded in Lakeside and Old Town.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended repairing or replacing PU-L2 before the next monsoon. It recommended raising or
sealing electrical and clinical equipment on the H-4 ground floor. Mobile pumps should be placed at the D-11
sump before forecast cyclones, not after a fixed pump fails. The use of S-1 and S-3 instead of S-7 worked, and
transport from Lakeside to those shelters should be planned in advance.
