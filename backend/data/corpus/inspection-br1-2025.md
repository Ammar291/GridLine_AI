This report records the principal inspection of the Kalinadi Bridge (BR-1) carried out by the NMC Engineering
Department on 12 November 2025, after the 2025 monsoon. The inspection combined a visual survey of the deck,
girders and bearings, an underwater inspection of every pier by divers, and a survey of bed levels around each
pier. Principal inspections of BR-1 are made every two years; the previous one was in November 2023.

## 1 The structure

BR-1 carries Kalinadi Bridge Road (RD-03) across the Kalinadi (R-1) from Old Town (Z-OT) to New Colony (Z-NC).
Built in 1962, it is a reinforced concrete bridge 310 m long, of eight spans on seven river piers and two
abutments, with a two-lane carriageway and footpaths. The vertical clearance is 6.2 m and the waterway area
1,850 m2. The river gauge RV-01, at which the Kalinadi stage thresholds of dmp-2024 are read, is mounted on the
bridge. BR-1 was the only road crossing of the Kalinadi until the Eastern Bridge (BR-2) opened in 2023.

## 2 Scour at pier 3

Pier 3 stands in the main river channel. Scour undermining its foundation was first found by divers after the
2021 flood (HI-2021-FL-01), when the Kalinadi reached its record stage of 5.8 m. This inspection measured the
scour hole at the upstream nose of pier 3 at 2.4 m below the surrounding bed, against 1.9 m in November 2023,
exposing the top 1.1 m of the well foundation on its upstream side. The permanent repair recommended after the
2021 flood has not been carried out. Piers 2 and 4 show local scour of less than 0.5 m; the other piers and both
abutments show none. The scour risk of the bridge remains rated high.

## 3 Superstructure and deck

The main girders and the deck slab are in fair condition. Spalled concrete with exposed reinforcement was
recorded on the soffit of spans 4 and 5, below deck drains that discharge onto the girders. The asphalt
surfacing is cracked but serviceable, and the parapets are complete. No cracking was found in the girders over
pier 3, and levels taken along the deck showed no settlement of pier 3 since the 2023 inspection. The footpaths
are uneven at two joints and were marked for repair.

## 4 Bearings, joints and waterway

Two of the four expansion joints are worn and let water through onto the bearings of piers 2 to 4, whose
elastomeric pads show surface cracking but no loss of seating. The bearings on the other piers and on the
abutments are sound. No debris was lodged against the piers at the time of the inspection. Floating debris that
lodged against the upstream noses of piers 3 and 4 during Cyclone Meher in October 2024 (HI-2024-CY-01) was
removed after that event, and the divers found no new damage to the pier faces from it.

## 5 Flood closures

BR-1 is closed to all traffic when the Kalinadi stage at RV-01 reaches 5.0 m (pol-flood-response-2024 section 5
and pol-road-closure-2021 section 4). Recorded closures: 30 hours in August 2012 (HI-2012-FL-01), after which a
structural check found no damage; 52 hours in September 2021 (HI-2021-FL-01), after which the bridge reopened
under a temporary load restriction once divers found the pier 3 scour; and 20 hours in October 2024 during
Cyclone Meher (HI-2024-CY-01), for high winds and debris against the piers with the river at 4.9 m. While BR-1
is closed, traffic between the banks uses the Eastern Bridge (BR-2) and the Eastern Highway Link (RD-12).

## 6 Load rating

The bridge is rated for vehicles of up to 40 t. This inspection confirmed the 40 t rating for normal river
conditions, taking the scour depth measured at pier 3 into account; the temporary restriction applied when the
bridge reopened in September 2021 is no longer posted. The rating does not cover the bridge during a flood, when
scour at pier 3 can deepen while the river is high. Vehicles heavier than 40 t cross the Kalinadi by the Eastern
Bridge (BR-2), which is rated for 70 t.

## 7 Condition ratings

| Element | Rating |
|---|---|
| Pier 3 foundation | poor |
| Other piers and abutments | fair |
| Girders and deck slab | fair |
| Expansion joints | poor |
| Bearings | fair |
| Parapets and footpaths | fair |
| Overall | fair |

The overall condition of BR-1 remains fair and its scour risk high, as recorded in the asset register. The
next principal inspection is due in November 2027.

## 8 Recommended works

The inspection recommends: permanent underpinning and scour protection of the pier 3 foundation; a diver
inspection of pier 3 after any event in which the Kalinadi stage at RV-01 exceeds the 4.2 m flood stage;
replacement of the two worn expansion joints; redirection of the deck drains away from the girders; and repair
of the spalled concrete on spans 4 and 5. None of these works had been programmed or funded at the date of
this report.
