## 1 Summary

Between 2012-08-18 and 2012-08-22 the Kalinadi (R-1) rose to a peak stage of 5.1 m on gauge RV-01, above
the 4.2 m flood stage. The Riverside flood plain (FP-1) in Z-RS was the worst affected, with spill into Old Town
(Z-OT) through the D-3 outfall and ponding in the Nandi Lake basin (Z-LK). About 12,000 people were affected
and 4,500 were evacuated. Two people drowned in Riverside and 15 were injured. BR-1 was closed for 30 hours.
The Old Town earth embankment, crest 214.0 m, held throughout. Total damage was estimated at 120 million.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

The event followed three weeks of above-normal monsoon rain, so soils across the valley were saturated before
it began. The heaviest 24-hour fall was 140 mm, with 260 mm over 72 hours and a peak intensity of about
28 mm/h at RG-03. RV-01 crossed the 4.2 m flood stage on 2012-08-17, passed 5.0 m in the early hours of
2012-08-19 and peaked at 5.1 m that afternoon. The barrage DM-1 passed high flows with all gates open. The
river stayed above flood stage for about four days and fell below 4.2 m on 2012-08-22.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

Riverside had no embankment on FP-1; the partial Bypass embankment was built later, in 2016. The Old Town
riverfront (FP-3) was protected by the 1978 earth embankment with a crest of 214.0 m. The D-3 Old Town main
drain, a brick culvert from 1890, was in poor condition. The D-8 Riverside outfall drain discharges through a
flap gate that shuts at a river stage of 4.0 m. The D-11 Lakeside pumped drain relied on three fixed pumps fed
from PS-4 once the river blocked gravity outflow through DM-2.

## 4 Impact

D-3 overflowed for about 40 hours, putting 0.3 m of water into streets near Bazaar Lane. In Riverside, RA-03
(Riverside Old Village) was inundated to 0.7 m for about 60 hours. The D-8 flap gate stayed shut for about 70
hours, and water ponded behind it. In Lakeside, RA-14 was flooded to 0.5 m and RD-07 was closed for 72 hours.
BR-1 was closed for 30 hours, cutting the direct link between Old Town and the south bank. About 1,400 houses
were damaged and 35 destroyed. There were two deaths and 15 injuries.

## 5 Response

The control room at PL-1 declared a flood emergency at 5.0 m on RV-01, and the Incident Commander ordered BR-1
closed. S-1 received about 700 evacuees from Riverside and S-3 about 1,300 from Old Town and Lakeside. Other
evacuees stayed with relatives. C-7 carried out boat evacuations in RA-03 and RA-14. C-2 cleared debris from
the D-3 inlets. C-1 supported search and rescue in Riverside. Police diverted traffic to the remaining river
crossing at the barrage road.

## 6 Outcome

The river fell below flood stage on 2012-08-22. BR-1 reopened after a structural inspection found no damage.
S-1 and S-3 closed within a week once evacuees had returned home. Both deaths happened in Riverside when people
went back to flooded houses before the all-clear was given. The Old Town embankment held with its crest at
214.0 m, and no breach was recorded. Silt and debris were cleared from D-3 and D-8 over the next month.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended evacuation triggers tied to RV-01 stage, with Riverside warned at 4.2 m instead of
after water entered homes. It also recommended pre-positioning C-7 boats in Riverside before the monsoon peak
and a condition survey of the D-3 culvert and its outfall. The Board asked for a feasibility study of flood
protection for FP-1. Public messaging should state clearly that residents must not return before the all-clear.
