## 1 Summary

Cyclone Taraka crossed the Kalinadi Valley between 2013-10-24 and 2013-10-26. WS-01 recorded sustained winds
of 105 km/h with gusts to 130 km/h. All ten zones were affected. Damage was heaviest in the open Nandi Lake
basin (Z-LK), where many houses have light roofs. About 25,000 people were affected, 1,200 were evacuated,
three people died and 41 were injured. PS-2 was out for 20 hours and PS-4 for 9 hours. Hill Road (RD-01) was
blocked for 14 hours. Damage was estimated at 85 million.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

The cyclone arrived late in the season after a normal monsoon. Soils were moist but not saturated. Rain
totalled 120 mm in 24 hours at RG-04, with a peak intensity of about 31 mm/h during the passage of the eye
wall. Winds at WS-01 went above 62 km/h early on 2013-10-24, peaked at 105 km/h (gusts 130 km/h) that night
and eased below 62 km/h by 2013-10-26. The Kalinadi rose to 3.8 m on RV-01, which is below the 4.2 m flood
stage, so river flooding was limited.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

Most 11 kV distribution in Lakeside and Riverside was on overhead lines. Z-SR, Z-OT and Z-MW were supplied from
PS-4, and so were the Lakeside pumped drain D-11 (fixed pumps PU-L1, PU-L2, PU-L3) and Lakeview Hospital H-4.
PS-2 served Riverside, Hillview and Tekri Heights from an unprotected site on the Riverside plain. Hill Road
(RD-01), the only paved access to Hillview and Tekri Heights, was lined with mature roadside trees. H-4 had
36 hours of generator backup.

## 4 Impact

Fallen trees brought down the PS-1 to PS-4 line, and PS-4 was out for 9 hours. During that time H-4 ran on its
generator and the D-11 fixed pumps, including PU-L1, stood idle. Water ponded in RA-14 but no houses flooded.
Feeder damage and a tripped transformer kept PS-2 out for 20 hours. Trees blocked RD-01 for 14 hours, which
isolated Hillview and Tekri Heights. RA-13 lost many roofs. About 2,100 houses were damaged and 60 destroyed.
Three people died from falling trees and debris.

## 5 Response

The Duty Officer issued a cyclone warning 36 hours before landfall. About 500 residents of RA-13 and RA-14
moved to S-7 and around 700 more went to relatives. C-6 restored the PS-4 incoming line first, because it
supplied the D-11 pumps and H-4, and then repaired the PS-2 feeders. C-3 and C-1 cleared trees from RD-01
working from both ends. C-5 treated the injured at H-1, and ambulances reached Hillview after RD-01 reopened.

## 6 Outcome

All power was restored within 20 hours. H-4 stayed on generator for 9 hours without interruption to patient
care. The D-11 pumps restarted when PS-4 returned, and the ponding in RA-14 cleared within a day. RD-01 reopened
after 14 hours. S-7 closed on 2013-10-27. Three deaths were recorded, two in Lakeside and one in Old Town, all
caused by falling trees or flying debris during the peak of the storm.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended pruning roadside trees along RD-01 and other evacuation routes before each cyclone
season. It asked for fuel checks and stock records for generators at H-4 and other hospitals. It also
recommended an assessment of a second power source for the D-11 pumping station, which was fed only from PS-4.
Crews should keep a pre-agreed order for restoring feeders, so that supplies to hospitals and drainage pumps
come back first.
