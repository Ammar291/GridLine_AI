## 1 Summary

On 2014-07-29 the hairpin section of Tekri Quarry Road (RD-11) failed on slope SL-TH-3 in Tekri Heights (Z-TH)
after heavy rain. Debris ran down onto Tekri village (RA-22), destroying 11 houses and damaging 18. Three
people died and 7 were injured. About 600 people were affected and 200 were evacuated. RD-11 was blocked for
9 days. Damage was estimated at 22 million. The slide happened in a single afternoon, and no warning had been
issued for the village before it failed.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

July 2014 was wet across the Kalinadi Valley. Rain gauge RG-01 on Tekri Hill recorded 240 mm over the 72 hours
before the failure, 165 mm of it in the final 24 hours. The peak intensity was about 38 mm/h, around two hours
before the slide. The residual soil over quartzite on SL-TH-3 is thin, and it was saturated by then. Runoff was
concentrated on the road surface. There was no river involvement, and the Kalinadi stayed below flood stage.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

RD-11 was an unpaved single-lane road serving the quarry area and Tekri village. The hairpin bends on SL-TH-3,
which has a mean angle of 36 degrees and a maximum of 48, had no side drains, catch pits or culverts. Road
runoff therefore spilled onto the slope at the outside of each bend. There were no soil moisture probes or
slope monitoring on Tekri Hill. The DM-3 check dams on Tekri Nala were not on the flow path of this slide.

## 4 Impact

About 2,500 m3 of residual soil and quartzite debris slid off two hairpin bends and ran into RA-22. It destroyed
11 houses and damaged 18. Three residents died inside destroyed houses and 7 were injured. RD-11 was blocked for
216 hours (9 days): two bends were buried and one section was washed out. Tekri village was reachable only on
foot from Hill Road (RD-01) during that time. About 600 residents were affected.

## 5 Response

The Duty Officer received the first call about 40 minutes after the slide and dispatched C-1 by Hill Road. C-1
recovered the three deceased and rescued 7 injured people, who were carried to RD-01 and taken to H-1. The
Incident Commander ordered 200 residents out of the run-out zone. About 150 of them stayed at S-6 and the rest
went to relatives. C-3 brought an excavator up RD-01 and cleared RD-11 from the western end over 9 days.

## 6 Outcome

RD-11 reopened to light vehicles on 2014-08-07 after a temporary regrading of the failed bends. S-6 closed after
three weeks, once the geologist's inspection allowed residents of undamaged houses to return. The 11 destroyed
houses were not rebuilt on the same plots. The failed bends were left without permanent drainage at the time
of this review, because funding for road drainage on RD-11 had not been allocated.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended side drains, lined cross-drains and catch pits at every hairpin of RD-11 so that
road runoff is carried off SL-TH-3. It recommended against rebuilding in the run-out zone below the hairpins
and asked for a rainfall-based warning for Tekri village using RG-01 readings. It also recommended that slope
stability be inspected by a geologist before each monsoon on the unpaved hill roads.
