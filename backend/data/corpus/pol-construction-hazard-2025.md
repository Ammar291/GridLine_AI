This policy is issued by the NMC Town Planning Department in consultation with NMC-DMC. Its conditions apply to
every construction permit issued or renewed after its effective date and are read into every such permit.

## 1 Purpose and scope

This policy sets the hazard conditions for building, road, drainage, quarry and utility works in Nandipur. It
applies to private developers, NMC departments and their contractors alike. It covers works on slopes, works
on flood plains FP-1 to FP-4, works within 50 m of a drainage channel and any works that change a drain,
culvert or bridge waterway. The construction halt rule of the Nandipur Disaster Management Policy (dmp-2024
section 5) applies in addition to this policy.

## 2 Hazard review

A hazard review by the Town Planning Department, with advice from NMC-DMC, shall be completed before a permit is
issued for works on any slope steeper than 25 degrees, on any flood plain, or within 50 m of a drainage
channel. The review shall record one of: approved, approved with conditions, or refused. Works shall not begin
while a review is pending. Conditions set by the review shall be written into the permit.

## 3 Halt rule on slopes above 25 degrees

On any slope steeper than 25 degrees, all excavation, cutting, filling and heavy machinery work shall stop when
the 24-hour rainfall at the rain gauge serving the site reaches 65 mm, or when soil saturation at any probe on
or below the site reaches 0.70. The permit holder shall monitor these readings daily from 1 June to 30
September. Work may resume only after inspection under dmp-2024 section 5 and written clearance by NMC-DMC.

## 4 Excavation on slopes

Excavation on slopes steeper than 25 degrees shall be carried out in benches no higher than 1.5 m, with each
bench supported or drained before the next is cut. No cut face shall be left unsupported for more than 14 days.
Permanent retaining structures required by the permit shall be completed before the monsoon season stated in
the permit. Spoil shall not be placed on the slope below the excavation.

## 5 Hydraulic review of drain and culvert works

Any works that change the cross-section, lining, gradient or waterway area of a drain, culvert or bridge
opening, including works carried out as part of a road project, shall require a hydraulic review by the NMC
Drainage Division before they begin. The review shall compare the new capacity with the design flow of the
channel and shall be notified to NMC-DMC. Works that reduce the capacity of a channel shall not proceed without
the approval of the Emergency Operations Director.

## 6 Works near drainage channels

No spoil, stockpile or site office shall be placed within 30 m of the bank of a drainage channel. Sites within
50 m of a channel shall install silt fences between the works and the channel before earthworks begin and shall
keep them in repair. The NMC Drainage Division may inspect such sites at any time and order the removal of
material from a channel at the permit holder's cost.

## 7 Construction on flood plains

On the flood plains FP-1 to FP-4, the plinth of every new habitable building shall be at least 0.6 m above the
25-year flood level stated by NMC-DMC for that flood plain. Electrical panels and generators shall be placed at
or above the plinth. Basements are not permitted on FP-1, FP-3 or FP-4. Every new housing scheme on a flood
plain shall provide drainage sized for its completed impermeable surface.

## 8 Dewatering

Dewatering discharge from any site into a drain shall not exceed 0.5 m³/s at any time, and shall be reduced or
stopped when the receiving channel reaches a flow ratio of 0.8 under pol-flood-response-2024. Discharge shall
pass through a settling tank. The permit holder shall record the discharge rate daily and report it to the NMC
Drainage Division weekly.

## 9 Monitoring and reporting

Permit holders on slopes steeper than 25 degrees or on flood plains shall submit weekly monitoring reports to
NMC-DMC covering excavation depth, support works, drainage, rainfall and any cracks or seepage observed. During
the monsoon season, sites on slopes shall be inspected daily by the permit holder's engineer. NMC-DMC shall have
access to any site and its records at any time.

## 10 Suspension and revocation

The Town Planning Department may suspend a permit when a condition of this policy or of the permit is not met,
and shall suspend it at once when a halt under section 3 is not observed. A suspended permit may be reinstated
after the condition is met and the site inspected. Repeated failure to meet conditions shall lead to
revocation of the permit.
