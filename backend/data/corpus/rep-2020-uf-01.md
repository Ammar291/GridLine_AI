## 1 Summary

On 2020-12-19 a solvent and paint warehouse south of Mill Road (RD-08) in Z-MI caught fire. There were several
explosions, and a dense smoke plume drifted west over RA-21 and RA-20. About 3,000 people were evacuated. One
warehouse worker died and 22 people were injured, many with burns. The burn unit at H-5 filled all 16 of its
beds, and the overflow went to H-1. PS-3 was shut down for 6 hours as a precaution, which stopped WF-6. Damage
was estimated at 95 million.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

Conditions were dry and cool. No rain had fallen for over three weeks. WS-01 recorded a light easterly wind of
about 15 km/h, which carried the smoke plume west along RD-08 towards RA-21 and the labour lines at RA-20. Water
supply for firefighting came from hydrants on RD-08 and the FS-2 water tender. There were no hydrological
factors in the event.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

The warehouse held drums of solvent above its licensed quantity. It stood about 400 m east of PS-3, which feeds
Mill Road and the WF-6 sewage treatment plant (4 hours of backup power). H-5 had the city's only burn unit, with
16 beds. FS-2, about 1.2 km away, held the only foam tender (FT-07). RD-08 was the main access route to the
site.

## 4 Impact

One worker died inside the warehouse, and 22 people were injured. Burn casualties filled all 16 burn beds at H-5,
and 5 patients went to H-1. PS-3 was shut down for 6 hours as a precaution, and WF-6 stopped for the same
period. RD-08 was closed for 14 hours. About 3,000 residents of RA-21 and RA-20 were evacuated. Fifteen nearby
houses were damaged by blast and heat. The warehouse was destroyed.

## 5 Response

FS-2 arrived about 8 minutes after the call. It used FT-07 foam on the burning solvents and kept water from its
pumper and tender on the neighbouring stores. FS-1 sent two pumpers and its water tender to support. The Incident
Commander ordered evacuation downwind. S-8 took about 350 people and others went to relatives and S-4. C-5 ran
burn triage at the RD-08 cordon. C-6 isolated PS-3 on the advice of the Chief Fire Officer.

## 6 Outcome

The fire was brought under control after about 7 hours and put out after about 11 hours. PS-3 was re-energised
after 6 hours once the smoke had cleared, and WF-6 resumed treatment with no reported overflow. Evacuees
returned home overnight. The five patients transferred to H-1 were treated there. The warehouse operator's
licence was suspended pending an inquiry into the excess solvent stock.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended stricter licensing of solvent storage in Z-MI, with minimum separation distances
from PS-3, housing and hospitals. It asked for a mutual-aid burn-care plan between H-5 and H-1, with transfer
triggers and transport arranged in advance. It also asked for a written protocol for shutting down PS-3 during
nearby fires, including notice to WF-6. FT-07 should be kept available whenever FS-2 has an appliance
committed elsewhere.
