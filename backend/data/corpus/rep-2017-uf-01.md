## 1 Summary

On the night of 2017-11-02 a fire started in a shop-house on Bazaar Lane (RD-05) in Old Town (Z-OT) and spread
through the tightly packed Bazaar quarters (RA-07). Six people died and 30 were injured. The fire destroyed 38
houses and damaged 90. About 2,500 people were affected and 600 were evacuated. The 4 m wide lane stopped the
ladder appliance FT-03 from reaching the fire. The fire burned for 9 hours before it was put out. Damage was
estimated at 75 million.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

The fire occurred during a dry spell after the monsoon. There had been no rain for 18 days and none fell on the
night. WS-01 recorded a light wind of about 12 km/h, which did not drive the spread much. The fire spread mainly
through shared timber roofs and stored goods. It was festival season, and several shop-houses held fireworks
and bottled cooking fuel, which caused explosions during the first two hours.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

RD-05 Bazaar Lane is a single lane 4 m wide, and FT-03 needs at least 6.0 m of road width. The lane's eastern
approach crosses D-3 on BR-5, a heritage arch bridge rated at 10 t. Hydrants in the quarter were supplied from
WF-4. FS-1 is about 1.2 km away by road. The foam and water-tender support at FS-2 in Mill Road had to cross the
river on BR-1, the only road crossing then.

## 4 Impact

The fire destroyed 38 shop-houses in RA-07. It damaged 90 buildings, including houses on the edge of the Old
Town core (RA-06). Six residents died, trapped on upper floors that FT-03 could not reach. Thirty people were
injured. About 600 residents of the adjoining blocks were evacuated, and about 450 of them stayed at S-2 for
three nights. RD-05 was closed for 30 hours. About 2,500 people lost homes, stock or access to work.

## 5 Response

FS-1 arrived about 14 minutes after the call. Pumpers laid hose from both ends of RD-05. FT-03 was set up on the
Market Ward side but could not reach the burning blocks, so crews used hand-carried ladders instead. The Chief
Fire Officer called FS-2, whose pumper and water tender came via BR-1 and arrived after about 35 minutes. C-5
ran triage at PL-1, and C-1 searched collapsed structures once the fire was under control.

## 6 Outcome

The fire was declared out after 9 hours. The six deaths were confirmed the following morning. Of the 30
injured, 11 were admitted to H-1. S-2 closed after three nights, when evacuees moved to relatives or temporary
accommodation. The 38 destroyed shop-houses were cleared over two months. RD-05 reopened after 30 hours with
barricades around unsafe structures that remained for several weeks.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended pre-planned access routes for Bazaar Lane and other lanes narrower than 6 m, and
adding a mini-pumper that can work in lanes around 3 m wide. It asked for dry-riser standpipes along RD-05 so
that crews do not depend on long hose lays. It also recommended a ban on storing fireworks and bulk cooking fuel
in the Bazaar quarters, and yearly fire drills with residents.
