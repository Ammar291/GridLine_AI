## 1 Summary

Cyclone Viraj crossed Nandipur between 2019-05-03 and 2019-05-05, before the monsoon. WS-01 recorded sustained
winds of 110 km/h with gusts of 140 km/h. All zones were affected, and Riverside (Z-RS) was hit hardest. PS-2
was out for 26 hours. Riverside Community Hospital (H-2) ran on its generator for 12 hours until it reached its
limit. About 30,000 people were affected and 2,500 were evacuated to S-1, S-2 and S-3. Two people died and 36
were injured. Damage was estimated at 110 million.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

Soils were dry and the Kalinadi was low at landfall, peaking at 3.2 m on RV-01. RG-03 recorded 85 mm in 24 hours
with a peak intensity of about 22 mm/h, and there was no significant flooding. Winds at WS-01 went above
62 km/h on the evening of 2019-05-03, went above 89 km/h overnight and peaked at 110 km/h (gusts 140 km/h) early
on 2019-05-04. They fell below 62 km/h on 2019-05-05. Trees were in full leaf, so more of them came down.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

PS-2 sits on the Riverside plain and feeds Riverside, Hillview and Tekri Heights, mostly over overhead lines.
H-2 depends on PS-2 and had 12 hours of generator backup. The Riverside raw-water intake WF-2 also depends on
PS-2 and had no backup power. RD-01 is the only paved road to Hillview and Tekri Heights. Informal housing in
RA-05 along D-8 had light sheet roofs.

## 4 Impact

PS-2 was out for 26 hours because of downed feeders and a damaged transformer bushing. H-2 ran on its generator
for 12 hours until the fuel ran out. It then had no power for about 2 hours before a mobile generator was
connected. WF-2 stopped for 26 hours. RD-01 was blocked for 10 hours. RA-05 lost most of its roofs. About 3,200
houses were damaged and 85 destroyed. Two people died under falling structures.

## 5 Response

Evacuation of kutcha housing began 24 hours before landfall. S-1 took about 750 people, S-2 about 550 and S-3
about 1,200. C-6 began repairs at PS-2 as soon as wind speeds allowed. When H-2 reported its fuel was nearly
gone, the Incident Commander ordered six critical patients transferred to H-1. C-6 then brought in a mobile
generator. Road crews and C-1 cleared RD-01. Water tankers supplied Riverside while WF-2 was down.

## 6 Outcome

PS-2 was fully restored after 26 hours, and H-2 returned to mains power. The six transferred patients recovered
at H-1. WF-2 resumed supply once PS-2 was back. S-1, S-2 and S-3 closed by 2019-05-07. Both deaths happened in
Riverside during the peak winds. Rebuilding of roofs in RA-05 took about two months and was supported by an NMC
materials grant.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended increasing fuel storage at H-2 so its generator can run well beyond 12 hours. It
also recommended pre-arranged mobile generators for H-2 and WF-2 whenever PS-2 is at risk, and pruning
roadside trees along RD-01. The Board asked the power utility to consider underground cables for the PS-2
feeders serving H-2. Hospital fuel levels should be reported to the control room every six hours during a
cyclone.
