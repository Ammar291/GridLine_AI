This policy sets how residents of Nandipur are moved out of danger. It applies to every evacuation ordered by
NMC, whatever the hazard, and to the departments, police stations and crews that carry it out.

## 1 Purpose and scope

This policy sets the conditions for an evacuation order, the routes to be used from each zone, the order in which
residents are moved and the arrangements for transport, assembly and return. Routes are chosen so that residents
move away from the hazard and towards shelters that are open under the shelter activation policy. The policy
applies to whole zones and to parts of zones such as a single slope, settlement or flood plain.

## 2 Evacuation orders

An evacuation order shall be approved by the Emergency Operations Director and shall name the area, the hazard,
the route, the assembly points and the destination shelters. The order shall be issued through the city control
room, sirens, the 112 service, police stations and ward volunteers. Where a zone is at critical under the
Disaster Management Policy and life is at immediate risk, police and rescue crews may move residents at once and
seek the order afterwards.

## 3 Hillview and Tekri Heights

Hillview (Z-HV) and Tekri Heights (Z-TH) shall be evacuated via Hill Road (RD-01) only, which is the only paved
access from the city. Residents shall be moved downhill to Riverside and on by RD-10 to Civil Lines. Where RD-01
is blocked or at risk of blockage, residents shall be moved uphill to the Tekri Devi Temple Hall (S-6) and held
there until the road is reopened. Residents of the settlement along D-7 and those below active cut slopes shall
be moved first.

## 4 Riverside

Riverside (Z-RS) shall be evacuated via the Riverside Bypass (RD-02) towards Old Town and via Civil Lines Road
(RD-10) towards Civil Lines. When the Kalinadi stage at RV-01 approaches 5.6 m, at which RD-02 floods, all
remaining movement shall use RD-10. Residents of the D-8 settlement and of the Riverside Old Village on the flood
plain FP-1 shall be moved before other residents.

## 5 Lakeside

Lakeside (Z-LK) shall be evacuated via the Lake Causeway (RD-14) towards New Colony and via the Lakeside Ring
Road (RD-07) to reach it. Because RD-07 floods at a Kalinadi stage of 4.5 m, the evacuation of Lakeside shall be
completed, or residents shall be moved to high ground within the zone, before the stage reaches 4.5 m. The Boat
Rescue Unit (C-7) shall be positioned at the Ring Road settlement whenever a Lakeside evacuation is ordered.

## 6 New Colony and Mill Road Industrial

New Colony (Z-NC) shall be evacuated north across the Kalinadi via the Kalinadi Bridge Road (RD-03) and bridge
BR-1 while BR-1 is open, or east via Mill Road (RD-08) and the Eastern Highway Link (RD-12) once the Eastern
Bridge (BR-2) is open to traffic. Mill Road Industrial (Z-MI) shall be evacuated via RD-08 and RD-12. Because
BR-1 closes at a stage of 5.0 m, movements across it shall be completed before that stage.

## 7 Old Town, Market Ward, Station Road and Civil Lines

Old Town (Z-OT) shall be evacuated north via the Market Ward roads to Civil Lines; Market Ward (Z-MW) and Station
Road (Z-SR) via Station Road (RD-04) to the Station Road Sports Complex (S-3) or to Civil Lines. Civil Lines
(Z-CL) stands on higher ground and receives evacuees; it shall be evacuated only for a fire or a local
hazard, using RD-10 or RD-04 away from the hazard.

## 8 Vulnerable-first order

Within every evacuation, residents shall be moved in this order: hospital patients and residents of care homes;
persons with disability, the elderly over 65, pregnant women and children under 5 with their carers; schools in
session; residents of informal settlements and of the lowest ground; all other residents. Police stations and
ward volunteers shall keep lists of households needing assistance and shall visit them first.

## 9 Transport and assembly

NMC shall provide buses from assembly points for residents without transport. Assembly points shall be on high
ground on the named route and shall be listed in the order. Ambulances shall be reserved for patients and
persons who cannot travel by bus. Pets and light belongings may be taken; vehicles shall not be taken on a
route that the Incident Commander has reserved for buses.

## 10 Return

Residents may return when the Emergency Operations Director lifts the order, after the zone has returned to
watch or normal and the roads, power and water supply have been checked. Return shall be organised in the
reverse order of priority where housing is damaged, and the NMC Engineering Department shall inspect buildings
in affected areas before residents re-enter.
