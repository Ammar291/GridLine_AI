## 1 Summary

Between 2016-09-08 and 2016-09-12 the Nandi Lake basin (Z-LK) flooded. The Kalinadi stood at 4.6 m on RV-01, so
the DM-2 regulator was closed and D-11 could only drain the basin by pumping. Fixed pump PU-L1 then failed, and
effective D-11 capacity fell from 12 to 7 m3/s. About 9,000 people were affected and 2,200 were evacuated. One
person drowned in RA-14 and 9 were injured. The ground floor of H-4 flooded to 0.4 m, and water reached 0.2 m
inside shelter S-7. Damage was estimated at 48 million.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

RG-04 recorded 210 mm over 72 hours, with 95 mm in the heaviest 24 hours and a peak intensity of about 24 mm/h.
The Nandi Lake was already high after the monsoon. The Kalinadi passed its 4.2 m flood stage on 2016-09-08 and
peaked at 4.6 m on 2016-09-09. CL-D11 at DM-2 showed the channel level rising steadily from the afternoon of
2016-09-08 as inflow exceeded pumped outflow. The river fell below 4.2 m on 2016-09-11.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

D-11 has a design capacity of 12 m3/s: three fixed pumps (PU-L1, PU-L2, PU-L3) of 2.5 m3/s each, plus 4.5 m3/s
of gravity outflow through DM-2. That gravity outflow is only available when the river is low. With the river
high, the DM-2 gates were closed to prevent backflow, so the pumps were the only outlet. The pumps were fed
from PS-4. PU-L1 was overdue for its overhaul. H-4 had its generator and switchgear at ground level.

## 4 Impact

PU-L1 seized after 14 hours of continuous running and stayed out for about 110 hours. D-11 surcharged for 96
hours at an effective capacity of about 7 m3/s. RA-14 flooded to 0.8 m and RA-13 to 0.4 m. RD-07 was closed
for 96 hours. H-4's ground floor took 0.4 m of water for about 50 hours. S-7 opened for about 600 people, but
0.2 m of water entered the hall. About 850 houses were damaged and 6 destroyed.

## 5 Response

The Executive Engineer (Drainage) had C-2 bring mobile pumps PU-M1 and PU-M2 from the Old Town depot to the D-11
sump. Each adds 0.6 m3/s. C-7 evacuated RA-14 by boat, and police used RD-14 to move evacuees towards New
Colony. When water entered S-7, evacuees were moved to its raised stage area, and later 300 of them went to S-3.
H-4 moved patients to upper floors and kept its generator running on sandbag protection.

## 6 Outcome

The river fell below flood stage on 2016-09-11. DM-2 was reopened and gravity outflow resumed, and the basin had
drained by 2016-09-12. One man drowned in RA-14 while crossing a flooded lane. PU-L1 was rebuilt with new
bearings and returned to service in November 2016. H-4 resumed full ground-floor services after cleaning. S-7
needed a week of cleaning before it could be used again.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended an overhaul of all three D-11 fixed pumps before each monsoon, with run-hour
records kept. It asked for standby power and a spare-parts stock at the D-11 station. The Board recommended
reviewing whether S-7, which sits on FP-4, is suitable as a flood shelter. H-4 should be asked to raise its
electrical equipment above ground-floor level. A lake-level gauge for the Nandi Lake was also proposed.
