This policy applies the flood and flash flood thresholds of the Nandipur Disaster Management Policy (dmp-2024,
sections 4.3 and 4.4) to the drainage network, gates, bridges and critical facilities of Nandipur.

## 1 Purpose and scope

This policy sets the actions NMC departments shall take when the Kalinadi (R-1), Nandi Lake (R-3) or any
drainage channel threatens to flood a zone. It covers the riverine zones Riverside (Z-RS), Old Town (Z-OT),
Lakeside (Z-LK), New Colony (Z-NC) and Mill Road Industrial (Z-MI), and every zone drained by a monitored
channel, including Hillview (Z-HV) through D-7. It is read together with dmp-2024, pol-road-closure-2021,
pol-shelter-activation-2024 and sop-pump-deployment-2022.

## 2 Flood bands

The Duty Officer shall band each zone using the flood index (watch ≥ 0.30, warning ≥ 0.50, critical ≥ 0.70) and
the Kalinadi stage at RV-01 (watch ≥ 4.2 m, warning ≥ 5.0 m, critical ≥ 5.5 m) as set in dmp-2024 section 4.3.
For flash floods the one-hour rainfall thresholds of 30 mm/h (watch) and 50 mm/h (warning) apply. The highest
band triggered by any of these readings applies to the zone.

## 3 Channel flow monitoring

For every drainage channel with a level sensor or a flow estimate, the Duty Officer shall compute the channel
flow ratio, defined as the estimated flow divided by the channel's current capacity (not its design capacity).
A channel is at watch when the channel flow ratio is ≥ 0.8 and is treated as overflowing when the ratio is
≥ 1.0. At watch, the Incident Commander shall be informed and the drainage crew C-2 placed on standby. At 1.0
or above, the zones upstream and downstream of the channel shall be raised to flood warning and road checks
ordered under pol-road-closure-2021.

## 4 Gates and regulators

The flap gate at the outfall of the Riverside outfall drain (D-8) closes when the Kalinadi stage at RV-01
reaches 4.0 m; from that moment D-8 cannot discharge and ponding in Riverside (Z-RS) shall be expected and
monitored. The Nandi Lake Regulator (DM-2) shall be closed when the stage reaches 2.5 m. The Lakeside pumped
drain (D-11) discharges by gravity only while the stage is below 1.5 m; at or above 1.5 m the fixed pumps at
the D-11 station shall be run and their status reported to the EOC every hour.

## 5 Bridge closure

The Kalinadi Bridge (BR-1) on RD-03 shall be closed to all traffic when the stage at RV-01 reaches 5.0 m and
shall stay closed until the stage has fallen below 5.0 m and the bridge has been inspected for scour. Before
closing BR-1, the Incident Commander shall confirm that the Eastern Bridge (BR-2) route via RD-12 is open and
shall inform the fire stations FS-2 and FS-3 and all hospitals of the closure.

## 6 Hospitals and critical facilities

When the stage at RV-01 reaches 5.0 m, the Riverside Community Hospital (H-2) shall start evacuation planning:
it shall confirm receiving beds at H-1, list patients who cannot be moved quickly and report its generator fuel
and backup hours to the EOC. The Electrical Repair Crew (C-6) shall be placed on standby for the Riverside
substation (PS-2) and the operator of the Riverside intake (WF-2) shall prepare for shutdown. Evacuation of a
hospital requires an order from the Emergency Operations Director.

## 7 Mobile pump priority sites

Mobile pumps PU-M1 to PU-M4 (0.6 m³/s each) shall be allocated in this order unless the Incident Commander
records a reason to depart from it: first, the D-7 outfall and the Riverside low points it serves; second, the
TU-1 sump in Station Road (Z-SR) when the fixed pumps cannot keep the underpass clear; third, Lakeside (Z-LK)
along D-11 when the fixed pumps are short of capacity. Deployment follows sop-pump-deployment-2022 and requires
the approval of the Incident Commander.

## 8 Warning and dissemination

When a zone reaches flood watch, the Duty Officer shall issue an advisory to that zone. At flood warning,
ward volunteers and the police stations covering the zone shall be asked to warn residents on the flood plains
FP-1 to FP-4 door to door, starting with informal settlements along D-7, D-8, D-11 and D-12. Warnings shall
name the expected stage, the roads likely to close and the shelters that are open.

## 9 Stand-down and review

The flood band of a zone shall be lowered only in line with the hysteresis rule of dmp-2024 section 4.1. Gates
shall be reopened, pumps recovered and roads reopened in the reverse order of closure. Every channel that
reached a flow ratio of 1.0 or above shall be inspected for debris and damage within 72 hours and the result
recorded by the NMC Drainage Division.
