## 1 Summary

On 2018-07-21 a short, intense cloudburst hit Station Road (Z-SR). The D-5 culvert, which was partly blocked,
surcharged and water ran into the TU-1 rail underpass on RD-13. PU-T1, the only sump pump, failed and TU-1
filled to 2.1 m within about 40 minutes. A car was trapped inside and its two occupants died. About 3,500
people were affected and 300 were evacuated from RA-10. RD-04 was closed for 6 hours. Damage was estimated at
18 million.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

The storm came early in the monsoon, after moderate rain on the two previous days. RG-03 recorded 130 mm in 24
hours, most of it in under three hours, with a peak intensity of 78 mm/h between 17:00 and 18:00. The Kalinadi
stood at 2.9 m on RV-01, well below flood stage, so the D-5 outfall was free. The flood was caused by local
runoff from the urban catchment exceeding drain capacity. It was not caused by river levels.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

D-5 was rated at 8 m3/s, against a design capacity of 9 m3/s. It was about 20 % blocked by silt and refuse from
the dry season, which left an effective capacity of about 6.5 m3/s. TU-1 is 4.5 m below grade, and its sump
had a single pump, PU-T1, of 0.4 m3/s. The underpass had no automatic barrier or water-depth warning, and
closing it depended on police arriving at the site.

## 4 Impact

D-5 surcharged for about 5 hours and backed up into the TU-1 approaches. PU-T1's motor flooded and tripped, and
the underpass filled to 2.1 m. A car entered TU-1 as the water rose and was trapped, and its two occupants died.
RD-13 was closed for 26 hours and RD-04 for 6 hours. RA-10 next to D-5 flooded to 0.5 m for about 8 hours, and
300 residents were evacuated. About 260 houses were damaged. Five people were injured.

## 5 Response

The Duty Officer sent police to close RD-13 about 25 minutes after the first flooding reports, but by then the
car was already inside TU-1. C-1 recovered the vehicle with divers. C-2 brought mobile pumps PU-M3 and PU-M4 from
the Old Town depot and pumped out the underpass overnight. About 300 residents of RA-10 spent the night at S-3.
Police diverted traffic from RD-04 to RD-10 while Station Road was flooded.

## 6 Outcome

TU-1 was pumped dry after 20 hours and reopened after cleaning, 26 hours after it closed. PU-T1 was repaired and
back in service after 30 hours. RA-10 residents returned home the next day. The two deaths were the only
fatalities. D-5 was desilted in November 2018, which restored its capacity from about 6.5 to 8.0 m3/s. The work
is recorded in the infrastructure change log as CH-2018-01.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended desilting D-5 every year before the monsoon and a condition check after the first
heavy storm. It recommended installing a second sump pump at TU-1 and an automatic barrier that closes the
underpass when water rises, so closure no longer depends on police arrival. It also asked for a depth warning
sign at each TU-1 approach and a pre-monsoon test of the sump pumps.
