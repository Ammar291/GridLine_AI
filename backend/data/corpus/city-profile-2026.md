This profile is compiled by the NMC Planning Department from ward registers, building permits, utility records
and the annual population estimates. Figures are for mid-2026 unless stated otherwise.

## 1 Overview

Nandipur is a city of the Kalinadi Valley administered by the Nandipur Municipal Corporation (NMC). It covers
73.3 km² on both banks of the Kalinadi river, which flows west to east through the city, with Tekri Hill rising
to the north-east and the Nandi Lake basin to the south-west. The population was 344,700 in 2026, up from
263,300 in 2018. The city is divided into ten zones for planning and emergency management.

## 4 Vulnerable groups

Informal settlement population in 2026 by zone: Z-HV 2,600; Z-TH 700; Z-RS 9,400; Z-OT 2,600; Z-MW 1,100; Z-SR
5,200; Z-CL 300; Z-LK 7,300; Z-NC 3,800; Z-MI 4,600; a city total of 37,600, up from 27,400 in 2018. Children
under 5 make up between 8 % and 11 % of the population of each zone. Residents over 65 make up between 6 % and
12 %, the highest shares being in Old Town and Civil Lines. Persons with disability make up 2 % to 3 %.
Households average about 4.3 persons.

## 5 Housing and growth areas

New Colony has grown fastest, through the New Colony Housing Scheme: Sector 1 (RA-16, occupied from 2019),
Sector 2 (RA-17, from 2021) and Sector 3 (RA-18, from 2023), together housing about 48,000 people. In Hillview,
the Hillview Terrace Phase 1 apartment blocks were occupied in 2022 and Hillview Terrace Phase 2 is under
construction. Informal settlements lie along the drains D-7 (Hillview), D-8 (Riverside), D-11 (Lakeside Ring
Road) and D-12 (Mill Road), and along D-5 in Station Road. Old Town is fully built and its population is
declining slowly.

## 6 Key infrastructure

Rivers: the Kalinadi (R-1, gauged at RV-01 on BR-1), the Tekri Nala (R-2) and Nandi Lake (R-3). Main drainage
channels: D-2, D-3, D-4, D-5, D-7, D-8, D-9, D-11 (pumped) and D-12. Main roads: fourteen roads RD-01 to RD-14,
including Hill Road (RD-01), the only paved access to Hillview and Tekri Heights. River crossings: the Kalinadi
Bridge (BR-1) and the Eastern Bridge (BR-2). Dams: the Kalinadi Barrage (DM-1), the Nandi Lake Regulator (DM-2)
and the Tekri check dams (DM-3). Power: grid substation PS-1 (132/33 kV, 150 MVA) and four 33/11 kV substations
PS-2 to PS-5. Water: the Kalinadi treatment plant WF-1 (120 MLD), the Riverside intake WF-2 (40 MLD), the Tekri
Hill Reservoir WF-3 (12 ML), the Old Town tanks WF-4, the New Colony booster WF-5 and the Mill Road sewage plant
WF-6. Hospitals: H-1 (420 beds), H-2 (80), H-3 (60), H-4 (120) and H-5 (100), 780 beds in total.

## 7 Emergency services

Fire stations: FS-1 Central (Market Ward, 24 staff on shift), FS-2 Mill Road (18, foam capability) and FS-3 New
Colony (12), with ten fire appliances FT-01 to FT-10. Police: five stations PL-1 to PL-5, with the city control
room at PL-1. Ambulances: 14, based at the five hospitals. Crews: C-1 to C-8, 123 members in total, including the
Hill Rescue Team (C-4) and the Boat Rescue Unit (C-7). Mobile pumps: PU-M1 to PU-M4 at 0.6 m³/s each. Shelters:
S-1 to S-8 with a total capacity of 4,650 persons.
