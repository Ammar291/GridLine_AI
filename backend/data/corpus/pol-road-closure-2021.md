This policy is issued jointly by the NMC Roads Division and the City Police. It applies to every public road,
bridge and underpass within Nandipur.

## 1 Purpose and scope

This policy sets when a road shall be closed, who may close it, how traffic shall be diverted and when it may be
reopened. It aims to keep evacuation routes and emergency access open as long as they are safe and to stop
vehicles entering water, debris or fire zones. It applies to all roads RD-01 to RD-14, the bridges BR-1 to BR-5
and the tunnels TU-1 and TU-2.

## 2 Closure authority

The Incident Commander shall approve every planned road closure. A police officer or crew leader at the scene
may close a road immediately where there is a direct danger to life, and shall report the closure to the
Emergency Operations Centre (EOC) within 15 minutes. The EOC shall publish every closure and diversion and
inform the fire stations, hospitals and ambulance control.

## 3 Water on roads

A road or road section shall be closed to all vehicles when standing or flowing water on it reaches a depth of
≥ 0.3 m at any point, measured at the lowest point of the carriageway. Where depth cannot be measured, crews
shall treat water over the kerb as reaching 0.3 m. Evacuation routes shall be checked every hour during a flood
warning. Emergency vehicles may use a closed section below 0.5 m of depth only with the approval of the
Incident Commander.

## 4 River-stage closures

The following closures are triggered by the Kalinadi stage at RV-01: the Kalinadi Bridge BR-1 on RD-03 at
5.0 m; the Lakeside Ring Road (RD-07) at 4.5 m; the Lake Causeway (RD-14) at 5.2 m; and the Riverside Bypass
(RD-02) at 5.6 m. Closure crews shall be in position before the stage reaches the closure value, and diversions
shall be signed in advance. A road closed on stage shall reopen only after the stage has fallen below the value
and the road has been inspected.

## 5 Rail underpass TU-1

The Station Road rail underpass TU-1 on RD-13 shall be closed when water enters the underpass, when the
Station Road drain (D-5) surcharges or when either sump pump (PU-T1, PU-T2) is out of service during a flash
flood watch. The automatic barrier shall be lowered and a police officer posted at each end. TU-1 shall reopen
only after it is pumped dry and inspected.

## 6 Hill Road RD-01

Hill Road (RD-01) is the only paved access to Hillview (Z-HV) and Tekri Heights (Z-TH). Before the Incident
Commander closes RD-01 for any planned reason, the Hill Rescue Team (C-4) shall be pre-positioned above the
closure point with first aid and rope rescue equipment, and the Tekri Devi Temple Hall (S-6) shall be activated
for residents cut off by the closure. An unplanned closure of RD-01 by landslide debris shall be treated as a
critical incident for both zones.

## 7 Landslide debris and fallen trees

Any road on which debris, rockfall or a fallen tree blocks more than one lane shall be closed until cleared by
the Road Maintenance Crew (C-3). During a landslide warning, the Tekri Quarry Road (RD-11) shall be closed to
all but emergency traffic, and the cut slopes along RD-01 shall be inspected before each reopening. Crews shall
not clear debris below an active slide until C-4 has inspected the slope.

## 8 Fire-access lanes

Lanes narrower than 6.0 m cannot admit the ladder appliance FT-03, which needs 6.0 m of clear width. Parking and
street trading shall be kept off the full width of Bazaar Lane (RD-05, 4 m wide) and similar lanes in Old Town
(Z-OT). During a fire in such a lane, the fire service shall use its narrowest appliance or hose relays from the
nearest road at least 6.0 m wide, and adjacent roads shall be closed to all traffic.

## 9 Reopening

A closed road may reopen when the cause has cleared, the surface and any bridge or culvert on it have been
inspected, and the Incident Commander has approved. Reopening shall be published by the EOC. Roads shall be
reopened in order of priority: evacuation routes and hospital access first, then bus routes, then other roads.
