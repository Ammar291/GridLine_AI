## 1 Summary

On 2025-07-19 a short, intense storm over the Hillview ridge sent about 31 m3/s down the D-7 Kalinadi drain.
The culvert at BR-4 under Hill Road has a capacity of 27 m3/s. D-7 surcharged at BR-4, and the CL-D7 gauge rose
to 2.9 m against a culvert soffit of 1.8 m. Water flooded the Kalinadi Drain settlement (RA-02) to 0.6 m and
closed Hill Road (RD-01) for 5 hours. About 2,400 people were affected and 400 were evacuated. Three people were
injured and nobody died.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

RG-02 recorded 96 mm in 24 hours, most of it in about two hours, with a peak intensity of 55 mm/h in the early
evening. There had been moderate rain over the previous week, so soils on the ridge were moist. The Kalinadi was
at 3.4 m on RV-01, below flood stage, and the D-7 outfall to R-1 was free. CL-D7 rose from 1.1 m to a peak of
2.9 m in about 70 minutes. The flooding was caused by channel flow, not river backwater.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

D-7 has a design capacity of 42 m3/s. In May 2025 the BR-4 culvert was rebuilt during the Hill Road widening as
a 2.4 x 1.8 m box, replacing the previous 3.2 x 2.4 m opening. That narrowing set the channel's current
capacity at 27 m3/s. Hill Road had four lanes on its lower 1.6 km. RA-02 lies along the upper bank of D-7, about
5 m from the channel. D-8 discharges into D-7.

## 4 Impact

D-7 surcharged for about 6 hours. With the culvert running full, water backed up upstream of BR-4, spilled into
RA-02 to 0.6 m for about 8 hours and crossed RD-01. RD-01 was closed for 5 hours. Downstream, low lanes of RA-03
took 0.3 m of water. About 310 houses were damaged and 2 destroyed. Three residents were injured by falls in
fast-flowing water.

## 5 Response

The Duty Officer closed RD-01 at BR-4 when CL-D7 passed 1.8 m and water reached the road. The Incident Commander
ordered 400 residents of RA-02 evacuated. S-5 was opened and received about 260 people, since RV-01 was well
below the 4.5 m floodplain shelter limit. C-2 deployed PU-M1 and PU-M2 at the D-7 outfall to lower water levels
in the Riverside reach. C-4 carried out house-to-house checks in RA-02.

## 6 Outcome

The storm passed within about 3 hours, and water levels at CL-D7 fell below the soffit after about 5 hours.
RD-01 reopened after 5 hours, following a check of the road surface at BR-4. RA-02 was clear of water after about
8 hours and residents returned the next day. S-5 closed on 2025-07-20. No one died. The Drainage Division
requested a hydraulic review of the BR-4 culvert.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended completing the hydraulic review of the BR-4 culvert that the Drainage Division had
requested. It also recommended an automatic alert when the CL-D7 reading approaches the culvert soffit of 1.8 m,
so that RD-01 can be closed and RA-02 warned earlier. Mobile pumps should be placed at the D-7 outfall when
RG-02 forecasts show intense rain. Debris screens upstream of BR-4 should be inspected after each storm.
