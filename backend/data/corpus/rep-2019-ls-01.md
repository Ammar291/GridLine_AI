## 1 Summary

On 2019-08-11 the Hill Road cut slope at km 2.1 (SL-HV-3) failed after prolonged monsoon rain. About 1,800 m3 of
debris crossed Hill Road (RD-01) and fell into the D-7 Kalinadi drain, blocking 45 % of the channel for 72
hours. RD-01, the only paved access to Hillview and Tekri Heights, was closed for 72 hours. D-7 overflowed and
D-8 backed up, so water ponded in Riverside, flooding RA-03 to 0.5 m. One person died, 4 were injured, about
6,000 were affected and 1,100 were evacuated.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

The failure followed ten days of monsoon rain. RG-02 on the Hillview ridge recorded 295 mm over 72 hours and
172 mm in the 24 hours before the slide, with a peak intensity of about 34 mm/h on the morning of 2019-08-11.
The Kalinadi stood at 3.9 m on RV-01, just below the 4.2 m flood stage, so D-7 could still discharge freely to
the river. CL-D7 at BR-4 showed a sudden rise after the slide as water backed up behind the debris.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

D-7 had a design capacity of 42 m3/s. Minor silting had reduced its current capacity to about 38 m3/s. SL-HV-3
is a 41-degree cut, with a maximum of 55 degrees, only 20 m from the D-7 channel. It had no mesh, rock bolts or
drainage. There were no soil moisture probes anywhere on the Hillview ridge, and no dedicated hill rescue team
existed. Hill Road was two lanes throughout. D-8 discharges into D-7.

## 4 Impact

The slide blocked both lanes of RD-01 and 45 % of the D-7 cross-section. D-7 overflowed upstream of the debris
into RA-02, flooding it to 0.4 m for about 20 hours. Downstream, D-8 could not discharge into the obstructed D-7
and surcharged for about 60 hours. RA-03 flooded to 0.5 m for about 30 hours. One motorist died when the slide
struck a car. Forty houses were damaged and three destroyed.

## 5 Response

Police closed RD-01 at both ends within an hour. The Incident Commander ordered evacuation of RA-02 and the
lowest parts of RA-03, 1,100 people in total. S-5 was opened and received 180 people, and the rest went to
relatives. C-1 recovered the motorist and searched the debris. C-3 put two excavators on RD-01 and in the D-7
channel. Hillview and Tekri Heights were supplied on foot over the debris until the road reopened.

## 6 Outcome

C-3 cleared enough debris from D-7 to restore flow at about 60 hours. RD-01 reopened to single-lane traffic
after 72 hours. The Riverside ponding drained within 30 hours once the channel was cleared. S-5 closed on
2019-08-14. The one fatality was the motorist. A geologist's inspection found tension cracks above the scar, so
the Board asked for the cut to be stabilised before the next monsoon.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended installing soil moisture probes on the Hillview slopes, with readings sent to the
control room. It also recommended forming a trained hill rescue team based near the foot of Hill Road. The km
2.1 cut should be protected with rockfall mesh and surface drainage. A debris-clearance plan for D-7 should
set out where excavators are pre-positioned during heavy rain, because a blocked D-7 affects Riverside drainage
through D-8.
