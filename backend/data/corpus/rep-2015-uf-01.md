## 1 Summary

On 2015-03-14 a fire broke out in the covered cloth market south of Market Ward Main (RD-06) in Z-MW. It spread
quickly through closely packed stalls full of textiles and destroyed 140 shops. Another 20 flats above shops
were damaged. About 1,200 traders and residents were affected and 12 people were injured. No one died and no
evacuation beyond the market blocks was needed. FS-1 fought the fire for 6 hours before it was contained.
Damage was estimated at 60 million.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

The fire happened in the dry season, after five weeks without rain. WS-01 recorded a steady north-westerly wind
of about 20 km/h, which pushed the fire south-east through the stalls. Humidity was low. No rainfall was
recorded on the day. There were no hydrological factors apart from the low level in the Old Town overhead tanks
(WF-4), which were part-way through a scheduled refill when the fire started.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

The hydrant main along RD-06 was supplied from WF-4, the Old Town overhead tanks, which hold 4 ML when full. On
the day they stood at about 30 % during a scheduled refill. The covered market had no sprinklers, and its
internal aisles were less than 2 m wide. FS-1 Central Fire Station is about 600 m from the market. The foam and
water-tender capacity of FS-2 was on the far side of the river, at Mill Road.

## 4 Impact

The fire destroyed 140 shops and damaged 20 flats above shops. Twelve people were injured, mostly with smoke
inhalation and burns to the hands. With WF-4 at 30 %, hydrant pressure on RD-06 could not supply two hose lines
together for about 4 hours. RD-06 was closed for 10 hours. Stock losses were heavy because most traders held
their goods in the market overnight. About 1,200 people lost their livelihood for several weeks.

## 5 Response

FS-1 arrived about 7 minutes after the call and committed all appliances. When hydrant pressure dropped, the
Chief Fire Officer asked FS-2 for its water tender, which relayed water from Mill Road for about 5 hours. The
water works raised WF-4 by switching the refill to full rate. C-5 triaged the injured on site and sent the more
serious cases to H-1. C-1 searched collapsed stalls, and police closed RD-06.

## 6 Outcome

The fire was contained after 6 hours and fully extinguished by the next morning. No one died. The 12 injured
people were treated at H-1, and 3 of them were admitted. The market was demolished and later rebuilt with wider
aisles. Traders operated from temporary stalls on RD-06 for about four months. WF-4 refill schedules were
changed so that refills take place at night.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended keeping WF-4 at a minimum of 60 % of storage during the dry season. It also asked
for standing arrangements for water tenders to be sent to market areas as soon as a fire is reported. A
fire-safety audit of covered markets in Z-MW and Z-OT was recommended, covering aisle width, sprinklers and
overnight storage of goods. Hydrant pressure on RD-06 should be tested quarterly.
