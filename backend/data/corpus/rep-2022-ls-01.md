## 1 Summary

On 2022-07-16 a construction cut on the Hillview Terrace Phase 1 site (PR-HT1, slope SL-HV-2) in Hillview (Z-HV)
failed after heavy monsoon rain. The cut was 4 m deep at 34 degrees and had been left unsupported for 3 weeks.
About 600 m3 of colluvium slid onto the working area and against the rear of the first occupied blocks of
RA-01. One construction worker died and 6 people were injured. All 350 residents of the occupied blocks were
evacuated. PR-HT1 was halted for 4 months. Damage was estimated at 35 million.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

It had rained for two weeks before the failure. RG-02 recorded 205 mm over 72 hours and 118 mm in the 24 hours
before the slide, with a peak intensity of about 29 mm/h. SM-03, the soil moisture probe on SL-HV-2 since 2020,
read 0.86 saturation on the morning of 2022-07-16 and had stayed above 0.70 for the previous two days. The
Kalinadi was at 3.4 m on RV-01. D-7, 140 m below the toe, was not affected.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

SL-HV-2 had a natural angle of about 28 degrees. The PR-HT1 excavation had steepened the cut to 34 degrees over a
height of 4 m, and it had stood without support for 3 weeks while the retaining wall design was pending. A
spoil heap sat at the crest next to Hill Road (RD-01). The first residential blocks of RA-01 were occupied
below the cut. SM-03 readings were logged but not linked to any rule for stopping work.

## 4 Impact

The slide buried part of the working bench, where one worker died and four were injured. Two residents of RA-01
were hurt when debris pushed against the rear wall of a block, and 12 flats were damaged. The spoil heap slipped
and blocked one lane of RD-01 for 24 hours. All 350 residents of the occupied blocks were evacuated. PR-HT1 was
closed for about 4 months.

## 5 Response

The site supervisor called the control room about 10 minutes after the failure. C-4 arrived from its base at the
foot of Hill Road within 20 minutes and, with C-1, rescued the trapped workers using rope rescue. C-5 treated
the injured, and four were taken to H-1. The Incident Commander ordered the occupied blocks evacuated, and S-1
received about 220 people. C-3 cleared RD-01. The Town Planning Department issued a stop-work order the same day.

## 6 Outcome

The worker was pronounced dead at the site, and the six injured people recovered. Residents returned to
undamaged blocks after 10 days, once a geotechnical engineer confirmed the debris had been stabilised.
Construction of PR-HT1 stayed halted for about 4 months while a 6 m retaining wall was built along the cut.
SL-HV-2 was reclassified from marginal to stabilised when the wall was completed on 2022-11-10.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended that construction cuts on hillside slopes not be left unsupported through the
monsoon, and that retaining structures be built before excavation below occupied buildings. It recommended
that work stop when soil moisture probes such as SM-03 show high saturation. Spoil should not be stored at the
crest of cuts or next to RD-01. Contractors should share probe readings with the control room during heavy rain.
