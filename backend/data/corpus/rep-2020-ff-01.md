## 1 Summary

On 2020-08-30 an intense storm over New Colony (Z-NC) surcharged the D-9 New Colony drain. Water flooded New
Colony Sector 1 (RA-16) and the western section of New Colony Avenue (RD-09) to 0.4 m. About 6,000 people were
affected and 500 were evacuated. Shelter S-4 opened with 0.1 m of water already inside. Nobody died and 2 people
were injured. About 420 houses were damaged by water. Damage was estimated at 14 million.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

RG-05 recorded 110 mm in 24 hours, with a peak intensity of 62 mm/h in the late afternoon. It was mid-monsoon,
so soils were moist and the water table on the former paddy land stood about 1.5 m below ground. The Kalinadi
was at 3.0 m on RV-01, below flood stage, so the D-9 outfall to R-1 was free. The flood came from local runoff
that exceeded channel capacity.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

D-9, a lined channel completed in 2019, has a design capacity of 20 m3/s for a catchment with 35 % impervious
cover. By August 2020, housing construction in the catchment had raised the impervious cover to about 44 %.
New Colony Avenue (RD-09) was partly built and open to local traffic ahead of its formal opening. Several
unfinished low points had no side drains. S-4 sits on FP-2 with its floor at street level.

## 4 Impact

D-9 surcharged for about 6 hours. RD-09 flooded to 0.4 m and was closed for 10 hours. RA-16 streets and ground
floors took up to 0.35 m of water for about 10 hours. RA-19 had 0.3 m in its lowest lanes. About 420 houses were
damaged. S-4 received about 400 people, but 0.1 m of water was already inside, so evacuees used the raised
platform and the upper room. Two people were injured by falls in flooded streets.

## 5 Response

The Duty Officer issued a flash-flood warning when RG-05 passed 50 mm/h. C-2 brought mobile pumps PU-M2 and PU-M3
from the Old Town depot across BR-1 and pumped the RA-16 low point. Police closed RD-09. About 500 residents of
ground-floor units were moved, 400 to S-4 and the rest to relatives. C-1 checked for trapped residents in RA-16
and RA-19.

## 6 Outcome

The water drained within about 10 hours after the rain stopped. Evacuees returned the next morning. There were
no deaths. D-9 showed no structural damage, but silt and construction debris were removed from two culverts.
RD-09 was completed with side drains at the low points and formally opened on 2020-11-30. S-4 was cleaned and
reopened for community use within a week.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended that the Drainage Division check D-9 capacity against the actual impervious cover
of its catchment, which had already passed the 35 % design figure. It recommended raising the floor of S-4 or
providing a raised hall. Low points on RD-09 should be graded and given side drains. Construction sites in the
catchment should be required to prevent silt and debris from entering D-9.
