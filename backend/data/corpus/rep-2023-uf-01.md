## 1 Summary

On 2023-04-08 a fire broke out in a railway goods warehouse beside the main line in Station Road (Z-SR), west of
the Rail Underpass Road (RD-13). FS-1 arrived 12 minutes after the call and contained the fire before it spread
beyond the warehouse. Four people were injured and nobody died. About 800 people were affected, and 200
residents of RA-10 were evacuated from the smoke. RD-13 was closed for 8 hours and rail services were suspended
for 6 hours. Damage was estimated at 26 million.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

It was a hot pre-monsoon day with no rain for four weeks. WS-01 recorded a westerly wind of about 18 km/h. The
wind carried smoke east over RD-13 and the Station Road settlement (RA-10), which is why residents were
evacuated even though the fire itself stayed within the warehouse. Humidity was low. No hydrological factors
were involved.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

The warehouse held packaged goods and drums of lubricant, stored together without separation. Hydrants on RD-04
were in working order and gave full pressure throughout. FS-1 is about 3 km away by RD-04. RD-13 runs through
the TU-1 underpass, which was dry. The railway had no agreed procedure with NMC for halting trains during
lineside fires.

## 4 Impact

The warehouse and its contents were destroyed. Six nearby houses had heat and smoke damage. Four workers were
injured, two with burns and two with smoke inhalation. RD-13 was closed for 8 hours and the western section of
RD-04 for 3 hours while appliances were staged there. Rail services were suspended for 6 hours. About 200
residents of RA-10 were evacuated for the afternoon and evening.

## 5 Response

FS-1 arrived 12 minutes after the call with two pumpers and its water tender, and connected to hydrants on
RD-04. The Chief Fire Officer asked the railway to halt trains, and it took about 25 minutes to confirm the
suspension. Police closed RD-13 and part of RD-04. C-5 treated the four injured and took two to H-3. About 150
of the evacuees went to S-3.

## 6 Outcome

The fire was contained within about 2 hours and fully extinguished after about 5 hours. No one died. Rail
services resumed after 6 hours, once the line had been inspected. RD-13 reopened after 8 hours and the evacuees
returned home the same evening. The warehouse operator was required to separate lubricant storage before
rebuilding.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended that lubricants and other flammable liquids in lineside warehouses be stored
separately from general goods. It asked for a written protocol with the railway so that trains can be halted
within minutes of a request from the Chief Fire Officer. It also recommended adding hydrants on the rail side
of the goods yard. The 12-minute response by FS-1 met the attendance target.
