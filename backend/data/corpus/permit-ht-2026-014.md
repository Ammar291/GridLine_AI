Permit HT-2026-014 is issued by the NMC Town Planning Department under the Construction Hazard Policy
(pol-construction-hazard-2025) following a hazard review with the outcome "approved with conditions". The
conditions below are binding on the permit holder and its contractors for the full duration of the works.

## 1 Permit particulars

Permit holder: Nandi Hills Developers. Project: Hillview Terrace Phase 2 (project id PR-HT2), residential
apartment blocks. Location: Hillview (Z-HV), on the Hillview Terrace slope SL-HV-1, mean slope angle 32°.
Site area: 18,500 m². Planned maximum excavation depth: 6.0 m. Nearest drainage channel: the Kalinadi drain
(D-7), whose bank lies 60 m from the site boundary at the toe of the slope. Monitoring sensors assigned to the
site: rain gauge RG-02 and soil moisture probes SM-01 (upper slope) and SM-02 (toe).

## 2 Excavation limits

Excavation shall not exceed a total depth of 6.0 m at any point. Excavation shall proceed in benches no higher
than 1.5 m, and each bench shall be supported, drained and inspected by the permit holder's engineer before the
next bench is cut. No cut face shall remain unsupported for more than 14 days. Spoil shall not be placed on
SL-HV-1 below the excavation. Any change to the excavation plan requires prior written approval of the NMC Town
Planning Department.

## 3 Rainfall and saturation halt conditions

All excavation, cutting, filling and heavy machinery work on the site shall halt when the 24-hour rainfall at
RG-02 is ≥ 65 mm, or when soil saturation at SM-01 or SM-02 is ≥ 0.70. The permit holder shall check these
readings at least twice a day, and every 3 hours when rain is falling. Work shall resume only after both
readings have fallen below these values, the site has been inspected by NMC-DMC or the Hill Rescue Team (C-4),
and written clearance to resume has been given.

## 4 Slope protection

The permanent retaining wall shown in the approved drawings at the downslope edge of the excavation shall be
completed before 2026-06-15. Until the wall is complete, temporary drainage shall intercept surface water above
the excavation and lead it to a lined outlet, and exposed cut faces shall be covered with sheeting when rain is
forecast. The permit holder shall keep vegetation on the slope outside the working area and replant disturbed
areas as works are completed.

## 5 Drain protection

No spoil, stockpile, fuel store or site office shall be placed within 30 m of the bank of D-7. Silt fences shall
be installed between the works and D-7 before earthworks begin and shall be inspected after every rainfall. Site
runoff shall pass through a settling pond before reaching any drain. Any material that enters D-7 from the site
shall be reported to NMC-DMC at once and removed at the permit holder's cost.

## 6 Monitoring and reporting

The permit holder shall submit weekly monitoring reports to NMC-DMC stating excavation depth reached, benches cut
and supported, progress of the retaining wall, drainage works, daily rainfall at RG-02, saturation at SM-01 and
SM-02, and any cracks, seepage or movement observed. From 1 June to 30 September the permit holder's engineer
shall inspect the slope daily and record the inspection. NMC-DMC shall have access to the site and its records at
any time.

## 7 Suspension and revocation

The NMC Town Planning Department may suspend this permit when any condition in sections 2 to 6 is not met, and
shall suspend it at once if work continues during a halt under section 3. A suspended permit may be reinstated
after the condition is met and the site is inspected. Repeated failure to meet conditions shall lead to
revocation. Suspension does not relieve the permit holder of the duty to keep the site safe and drained.
