This procedure governs how the Emergency Operations Centre (EOC) dispatches, tracks and recalls the city's
emergency crews.

## 1 Purpose and scope

This procedure applies to every dispatch of the crews C-1 to C-8 by the EOC, whether for monitoring, prevention
or response. It sets the checks to be made before dispatch, the crew to be sent for each kind of task, the
safety limits that apply in the field and the rules for relief and recall. Dispatch is approved by the Incident
Commander, who may delegate it to a sector lead.

## 2 Crew register

The crews are: C-1 Rescue Alpha (rescue, 24 members, based in Civil Lines); C-2 Drainage Crew Old Town (drainage,
14, Old Town depot, operates PU-M1 to PU-M4); C-3 Road Maintenance Crew (road, 16, Station Road); C-4 Hill Rescue
Team (hill rescue, 12, Riverside); C-5 Medical Response Team (medical, 10, at H-1); C-6 Electrical Repair Crew
(electrical, 9, at PS-1); C-7 Boat Rescue Unit (boat, 8, Lakeside); and C-8 New Colony Volunteer Team (volunteer,
30, New Colony).

## 3 Pre-dispatch road check

Before a crew is dispatched, the dispatcher shall check the current status of every road and bridge on the
planned route, including closures, water depth, debris and the Kalinadi stage relative to the closure values
for BR-1, RD-07, RD-14 and RD-02. A crew shall not be sent by a route with water of 0.3 m or more on it. The
dispatch record shall state the route, the expected arrival time and the task.

## 4 Assignment by hazard

For landslide tasks, C-4 shall lead, with C-3 for debris clearance. For channel overflow and ponding, C-2 shall
lead with the mobile pumps. For rescue from water, C-1 and C-7 shall be sent. For power loss at substations or
pumping stations, C-6 shall be sent. For casualties and shelter health, C-5 shall be sent. For door-to-door
warnings in New Colony and sandbagging, C-8 shall be sent. Only one crew shall lead each task.

## 5 Hillview and Tekri Heights

Hillview (Z-HV) and Tekri Heights (Z-TH) are reached only by Hill Road (RD-01). When a landslide warning is in
force, the Incident Commander shall pre-position C-4 above the lower Hill Road cuts before RD-01 is at risk, so
that a crew is available above any blockage. Dispatch to these zones shall include C-3 with an excavator when
debris is reported on RD-01.

## 6 Field safety

Crews shall not work on or below a slope at landslide critical, or in moving water deeper than 0.5 m, except for
life-saving operations approved by the Incident Commander. At cyclone warning, crews shall avoid work at height
and near trees and power lines, and at wind of 118 km/h or above they shall shelter in place. Each crew shall
report its position to the EOC every 30 minutes.

## 7 Relief and rest

No crew shall work more than 12 hours in a shift. The Incident Commander shall plan relief when a crew has
worked 8 hours and shall hold at least one crew in reserve when two or more zones are at warning. Crew members
showing signs of exhaustion or injury shall be withdrawn and replaced.

## 8 Recall

A crew shall be recalled when its task is complete, when conditions on its site exceed the safety limits of
section 6, or when the Incident Commander reassigns it. On return, the crew leader shall report the work done,
equipment used or lost, and any hazard observed, and the crew's status shall be set to available in the
register.
