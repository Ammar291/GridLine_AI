This policy governs the eight designated emergency shelters of Nandipur and applies the bands of the Nandipur
Disaster Management Policy (dmp-2024) to their activation.

## 1 Purpose and scope

This policy sets who may open a shelter, which shelter may be opened under which conditions, how many people it
may hold and when it shall be closed. It applies to the shelters S-1 to S-8, to the departments and
organisations that manage them, and to the Emergency Operations Centre (EOC). Shelters for cyclone, flood,
flash flood and landslide displacement are covered; fire displacement is handled case by case by the Incident
Commander using the same register.

## 2 Shelter register

The designated shelters and their rated capacities are: S-1 Civil Lines Community Hall (Z-CL, 800 persons,
generator), S-2 Govt Higher Secondary School Market Ward (Z-MW, 600), S-3 Station Road Sports Complex (Z-SR,
1500, generator), S-4 New Colony Community Centre (Z-NC, 400, on FP-2), S-5 Riverside Primary School (Z-RS, 300,
on FP-1), S-6 Tekri Devi Temple Hall (Z-TH, 200), S-7 Lakeside Marriage Hall (Z-LK, 500, on FP-4) and S-8 Mill
Road Workers' Club (Z-MI, 350, generator, on FP-2). NMC-DMC shall keep this register current.

## 3 Activation authority

The Incident Commander may open any shelter permitted under this policy when a zone it serves is at warning or
when displaced residents need cover. The Emergency Operations Director shall be informed of every activation
within 30 minutes. The Duty Officer shall confirm with the managing organisation that the building is
accessible, dry and staffed before residents are directed to it, and shall publish the list of open shelters
with every alert.

## 4 Floodplain shelters

Shelters located on a flood plain, namely S-4, S-5, S-7 and S-8, shall not be activated when the Kalinadi
stage at RV-01 is ≥ 4.5 m or when a flood warning is in force for the zone in which they stand. If such a
shelter is already open when either condition is met, the Incident Commander shall arrange the transfer of its
occupants to a shelter off the flood plain, S-1, S-2 or S-3, before the stage reaches 5.0 m. Floodplain
shelters may be used for cyclone and fire displacement when neither condition applies.

## 5 Tekri Devi Temple Hall

The Tekri Devi Temple Hall (S-6) in Tekri Heights (Z-TH) is reached only by Hill Road (RD-01). S-6 shall be
activated only while RD-01 is open and passable to buses and ambulances. If RD-01 is expected to close,
residents of Hillview (Z-HV) and Tekri Heights shall be directed to S-6 before the closure, and the Hill Rescue
Team (C-4) shall be pre-positioned. S-6 shall not be used during a landslide warning on the slopes it faces.

## 6 Occupancy cap

No shelter shall be filled beyond 90 % of its rated capacity. When a shelter reaches 90 % of capacity, the
shelter manager shall declare it full and the Duty Officer shall redirect arrivals to the nearest open shelter
with space. Occupancy shall be reported to the EOC every 2 hours. Where the combined capacity of open shelters
is insufficient, the Incident Commander shall request additional buildings from the Emergency Operations
Director.

## 7 Supplies and power

Each activated shelter shall hold drinking water for at least 3 days, food for 2 days, first aid kits and
bedding for its capacity. Shelters without a generator (S-2, S-4, S-5, S-6, S-7) shall receive a portable
generator within 6 hours of activation when mains power is lost. The Medical Response Team (C-5) shall visit
every open shelter at least once a day.

## 8 Closure

A shelter shall be closed when the zones it serves have returned to watch or normal for 24 hours, when its
residents can return home or when a condition in section 4 or 5 requires it. Residents shall be moved in
organised groups with transport, and the shelter shall be cleaned and inspected before it is recorded as
available again.
