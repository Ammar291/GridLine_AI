## 1 Summary

From 2021-09-12 to 2021-09-19 the Kalinadi produced the largest flood on record in Nandipur. It peaked at 5.8 m
on RV-01 on 2021-09-14. Riverside (Z-RS) was the primary zone, and Old Town, Lakeside, New Colony, Mill Road and
lower Hillview were also flooded. The Old Town embankment was overtopped by 0.3 m. PS-2, WF-2, WF-6 and shelter
S-5 were flooded. About 41,000 people were affected, 15,500 were evacuated, 9 died and 57 were injured. Damage
was estimated at 780 million.

## 2 Weather and hydrological conditions

It was the wettest monsoon in a decade, and the catchment was saturated before the event began. RG-02 and RG-03
recorded up to 210 mm in 24 hours and 410 mm over 72 hours, with a peak intensity of about 46 mm/h. The upstream
reservoir released water through DM-1 at up to 1,650 m3/s. RV-01 crossed 4.2 m on 2021-09-12, passed 5.5 m on
2021-09-13 and reached a record 5.8 m on 2021-09-14. LL-01 showed the Nandi Lake at its highest level since the
gauge was installed.

## 3 Infrastructure state at the time

The Old Town embankment on FP-3 had a crest of 214.0 m. FP-1 had only the partial Bypass embankment (214.5 m).
PS-2 and WF-2 stood unprotected on the Riverside plain, and H-2 depended on PS-2. D-8 drains through a flap
gate that closes at 4.0 m, and BR-1 closes at 5.0 m. D-11 depended on its three fixed pumps, fed since June
2021 from PS-5 on a platform raised 1.2 m. D-7 was carrying about 38 m3/s. S-5 is a school on FP-1.

## 4 Impact

The embankment was overtopped by 0.3 m, and RA-06 took up to 0.6 m of water. D-3 overflowed and TU-1 flooded.
PS-2 flooded and was out for 38 hours. WF-2 was out for 4 days. H-2 evacuated 36 patients to H-1. S-5 flooded to
0.9 m while occupied, and its 210 evacuees were moved to S-1. The D-8 gate stayed shut for 120 hours. PU-L3
overheated during 7 days of pumping. BR-1 was closed for 52 hours and RD-02 for 44. WF-6 flooded and
contaminated water in RA-16 and RA-17, both flooded to 0.6 m.

## 5 Response

The Incident Commander declared a city-wide flood emergency when RV-01 reached 5.0 m. S-1, S-2, S-3 and S-5 were
opened. When water entered S-5, C-7 and C-1 moved its 210 occupants by boat to S-1. C-4 evacuated lower Hillview
along D-7. C-2 committed all four mobile pumps, PU-M1 to PU-M4, to Old Town and TU-1. H-2 transferred 36 patients
to H-1 with C-5. C-6 worked to restore PS-2, and tankers supplied drinking water for 4 days.

## 6 Outcome

The river fell below 4.2 m on 2021-09-19. Nine people died: five in Riverside, two in Old Town and two in New
Colony. PS-2 was restored after 38 hours and WF-2 after 4 days. BR-1 reopened after 52 hours under a load
restriction once divers found scour at pier 3. PU-L3 was kept running at reduced output until the basin
drained. Cases of water-borne illness in New Colony were linked to the WF-6 overflow.

## 7 Lessons and recommendations

The Review Board recommended raising the Old Town embankment above the 2021 flood level and flood-proofing or
relocating PS-2 and WF-2. It recommended a review of shelters located on flood plains, starting with S-5,
because it flooded while occupied. PU-L3 should be replaced and BR-1 pier 3 repaired. Flood protection at WF-6
was also recommended, and H-2 should have a planned evacuation trigger linked to the PS-2 flood risk.
